Who we are

A home and a school, under one roof

Carunnai began as a small learning centre and has grown into a day-care school and residential home for children and young adults with disabilities – many of them orphans or abandoned by their families.

  • Day care and a safe residential home
  • Special education planned for each child
  • Therapy, life skills and vocational training
  • A refuge for children without family care
“Every child has unique potential. Our work is to find it, nurture it and help it shine.”

How we care

Everything a child needs to grow

Six promises we keep to every child who walks through our door.

01

A safe home

A caring place to live, day and night, for children who need it – including those without family care.

02

Learning every day

Special education planned around each child’s own pace, abilities and dreams.

03

Therapy & health

Speech therapy, physiotherapy, sensory work and medical and emotional support.

04

Skills for life

Self-care, tailoring, crafts, gardening and computer skills towards independence.

05

Love & belonging

Celebrations, outings and friendships – every child is part of one big family.

06

Families & community

Guidance for parents and outreach that builds inclusion and removes stigma.
