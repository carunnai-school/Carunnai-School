Our story

Our story of care

From one man’s visit to a disability camp to a home and school for children – this is how Carunnai began, and why we keep going.

How it began

It started with one visit

Carunnai Special School was founded by C. Ganesh after a visit to a camp for children with disabilities. Moved by what he saw, he started a small non-formal education centre. With his wife Revathy – a specialist in audiology and speech therapy – he built it into a complete support system for children with disabilities.

Today Carunnai cares for more than 85 students, including orphans. Personal education plans, therapy and practical training help each of them move forward – several students have completed secondary education through the National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS) and gone on to vocational training.

For children abandoned by their families, Carunnai is a refuge – a place of safety, security and happiness.

Our journey

From a small idea to a second home

1

Inspired by a camp

Founder C. Ganesh visited a camp for children with disabilities and saw how little structured support they had.

2

A small learning centre

Carunnai began as a non-formal education centre for children who had nowhere else to learn.

3

A school and a home

It grew into a full school and residential home – now caring for more than 85 students, including orphans.

4

Still growing

We keep adding vocational and therapy programmes, shaped by the needs of families and experts.

What drives us

Our mission & vision

Personalised education, complete care and emotional support – so every child can live with independence, dignity and full participation in society.

Our mission

  • Education that empowers – personalised learning that builds real skills.
  • Complete care – therapy, medical and emotional support for every child.
  • Families involved – tools and guidance for growth at home.
  • Independence – life skills and vocational training for self-reliance.
  • Community awareness – outreach that builds inclusion and removes stigma.

Our vision

  • Inclusive education – quality, tailored learning for every child.
  • Potential nurtured – each child’s strengths recognised and supported.
  • Dignity for all – respect that celebrates every child’s worth.
  • Skills for life – vocational training towards financial independence.
  • Full participation – accessible spaces and real opportunities in society.
