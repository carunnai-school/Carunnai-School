Learning for every stage of life

  • Basic educationLessons planned for each child’s development.
  • Speech & physiotherapyPlus sensory stimulation, one step at a time.
  • Hearing aid trainingFor students and their families.
  • Parent supportGuidance and counselling at home and school.
18 – 23

Skills & vocational training

Hands-on training that turns abilities into work and pride.

  • Tailoring & handicraftsSewing, coir rope, mat weaving and garlands.

Therapy & care

Support planned for each child

Our therapies and services are tailored to every individual and reviewed as they grow.

Speech therapy

One-to-one sessions to build communication, including sign language and speech devices.

Physiotherapy

Movement, strength and balance work tailored to each child’s body.

Hearing aid training

Helping students and families use and care for hearing aids.

Sensory integration

Structured activities that help children manage sensory sensitivities.

Visual stimulation

Guided activities that strengthen visual attention and understanding.

Life skills

Self-care, communication and social skills for daily independence.

Parent counselling

Guidance and training so families can support growth at home.

Residential care

A safe, caring home for children who need long-term support.
