Carunnai is a special school and residential home in Puducherry for children and young adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities – including orphans and children without family care. We focus on individual education, therapy and vocational training.

Carunnai was founded by C. Ganeshan after a visit to a camp for children with disabilities. It began as a small non-formal education centre. His wife Revathy, a specialist in audiology and speech therapy, guides the therapy work.

Yes. Alongside day care, Carunnai provides a safe home for children and adults who need long-term care – including children who have been abandoned by their families.

We support children with autism, Down syndrome, speech and language impairments, sensory processing difficulties and other intellectual disabilities.

  • Basic education planned for each child’s development
  • Therapies – speech therapy, physiotherapy and sensory stimulation
  • Life skills – communication, self-care and social skills
  • Parent support – guidance and counselling

Yes. Students aged 18–23 learn tailoring, gardening, handicrafts such as coir rope and mat weaving, and basic computer skills.

  • Employment support – sheltered employment or self-employment
  • Community integration – social and recreational activities
  • Ongoing therapy – speech, occupational therapy and emotional support

Yes. Speech therapy helps children communicate better, including through sign language and speech-generating devices where helpful.

Yes. We train students and families to use and care for hearing aids, so listening and communication improve.

Sensory integration therapy helps children manage sensory sensitivities and understand their surroundings through structured activities.
